This page will sometimes disappoint you on purpose. A good opinion should clarify what it does not know, not only what it claims.

Readers have asked for sharper lines and faster takes. Speed has its place — but not here, not at the cost of misstating a fact because the sentence sounded better.

> Civility is not manners; it is method.

We will name conflicts of interest, correct errors plainly, and publish responses when they meet the same standard we ask of ourselves. That is the contract.

If you disagree, write us. The best letters sharpen the next argument — they do not end it.
